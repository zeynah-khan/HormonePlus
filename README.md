# Hormone+
*A mobile health tracking app designed for users underserved by fertility-focused femtech — supporting unpredictable cycles, hormone-related conditions, and personalised understanding without paywalls!*

---

## Background

Digital menstrual and hormonal health tracking applications have become increasingly popular in recent years; however, many existing solutions are primarily designed around **fertility prediction and regular menstrual cycles**. This design focus often assumes consistency and predictability, which does not reflect the lived experiences of a large proportion of users.

Individuals with hormone-related conditions such as **Premenstrual Dysphoric Disorder (PMDD), Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS), perimenopause, and menopause** frequently experience irregular or unpredictable cycles, symptom variability, and non-linear patterns over time. Despite this, their needs are often insufficiently supported by mainstream femtech applications, which may deprioritise symptom understanding in favour of ovulation prediction or pregnancy planning.

Additionally, features designed specifically for menopause or non-fertility use cases are commonly restricted behind **premium paywalls**, further marginalising users who may already face barriers to accessing appropriate healthcare information and support.

This project was motivated by the need for a **more inclusive, transparent, and supportive approach** to hormonal health tracking — one that prioritises *understanding over prediction*. Rather than attempting to forecast cycles or enforce normative assumptions about regularity, the application focuses on helping users reflect on their symptoms, recognise patterns over time, and access contextual educational content aligned with their individual experiences.
